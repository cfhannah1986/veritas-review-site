*Veritas* is Latin for truth. We chose the name because it is the whole business: an honest, evidence-based review of your manuscript, not paid praise and not vague encouragement.

## What we do

Veritas Review reads your entire manuscript and returns a thorough written review covering characters, plot, pacing, continuity, theme, and style. Every significant point is tied to a specific passage in your text, so you can see exactly what we mean and where to act on it.

## How the review is made

We read your full manuscript carefully, then write a thorough review covering character, structure, pacing, and theme. Every significant point is tied to a specific passage in your text, so you can see exactly what we mean. Before delivery, each review goes through a final quality check for accuracy, tone, and fairness.

The result is a review you can trust: specific, quotable, and grounded in the book you actually wrote.

## What we believe

- An author deserves the truth about their book, kindly told.
- Every claim in a review should be traceable to the text.
- A review should leave you knowing exactly what to do next.
- Your manuscript is yours. It is never shared, sold, or used for anything but your review.
