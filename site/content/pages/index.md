<h2>What is inside every review</h2>
<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>Character analysis</h3><p>Who your characters are, whether they stay consistent, and where they earn or lose the reader.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Plot and structure</h3><p>How the story is built, where it sags, and which scenes carry the weight.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Pacing</h3><p>Where the book moves, where it drags, and what to tighten or expand.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Continuity check</h3><p>Contradictions, timeline slips, and details that do not line up, caught with quotes.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Theme and style</h3><p>What the book is really about, and how the prose serves or fights it.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Prioritized fixes</h3><p>The three to five changes that would improve the book most, in order.</p></div>
</div>
