We are open.

Veritas Review offers what its name promises: an honest, evidence-based review of your manuscript. Characters, plot, pacing, continuity, theme, and style, with every significant point tied to a passage in your text.

Here is what you get for the $14.99 launch price:

  • A full read of your manuscript (up to 100,000 words)
  • A written review delivered as PDF and DOCX within 48 hours
  • Specific, quotable notes, not vague encouragement
  • A prioritized list of the fixes that would help your book most
  • A human editor checking every review before it reaches you

We built this because most manuscript feedback is either too kind to be useful or too vague to act on. A review should leave you knowing exactly what to do next. That is the bar we hold ourselves to, on every manuscript.

If you have a book that is ready for the truth, order your review.
