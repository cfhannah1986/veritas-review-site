Our review engine analyzes characters, plot, pacing, continuity, and theme across the full manuscript, then verifies every factual claim against your text.

What is inside every review

Character analysis

Who your characters are, whether they stay consistent, and where they earn or lose the reader.

Plot and structure

How the story is built, where it sags, and which scenes carry the weight.

Pacing

Where the book moves, where it drags, and what to tighten or expand.

Continuity check

Contradictions, timeline slips, and details that do not line up, caught with quotes.

Theme and style

What the book is really about, and how the prose serves or fights it.

Prioritized fixes

The three to five changes that would improve the book most, in order.

We do not sell praise.

Some review services sell five stars. We sell the truth about your manuscript: what is working, what is not, and exactly where to fix it, with quotes from your own text. If your book is great, we will say so and show you why. If it is not ready, we will show you that too, kindly and specifically.
